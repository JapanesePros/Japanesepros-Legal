Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

JapanesePros (“we”) operates the JapanesePros mobile app. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Information we collect

  • Account: email and password if you create an account.
  • Learning data: progress, streaks, and study activity.
  • Microphone: audio when you use pronunciation or shadowing features.
  • Purchases: processed by Apple and RevenueCat (we do not receive your card number).

How we use it

To provide the app, sync progress, process purchases, and send study reminders if you enable notifications.

Third parties

We use Supabase (accounts/sync), RevenueCat (purchases), and Apple (App Store). Speech features may use third-party AI services.

Your choices

You can reset progress or delete your account in the app. You can deny microphone or notification permissions.
